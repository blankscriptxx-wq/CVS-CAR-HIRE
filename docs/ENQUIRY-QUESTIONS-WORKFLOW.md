# CVS Hire — Enquiry Questions Workflow (Aniro Knowledge Source)

This is the question flow the CVS website uses to build a quote and capture a
lead. The assistant should follow the **same** steps so chat and site collect the
same details. Pair this with **Chauffeur Pricing Guide** for the actual numbers.

**Principles**
- Friendly and brief — ask **one or two things at a time**, not a giant form.
- Always end with an **indicative "from" price** + capture **name and mobile
  and/or email** so the team can confirm and tailor it.
- Never quote a firm final price or invent discounts — the team confirms every
  booking.

---

## Step 0 — Capture who we're talking to (always first)

Before or early in the conversation, get:

- **Name**
- **Mobile** and/or **Email** (at least one)

> "Happy to help! Can I grab your **name** and a **mobile or email** so we can send
> your quote and follow up if we get cut off?"

(If they came in from a website quote/enquiry form, we already have this — don't re-ask.)

---

## Step 1 — Self-drive or chauffeur?

> "Are you looking to **drive it yourself (self-drive)**, or have one of our
> **chauffeurs drive you**?"

- **Self-drive** → go to **Section A**
- **Chauffeur** → go to **Section B**
- **Not sure** → ask the occasion; weddings/proms/airport/corporate usually mean
  chauffeur, a weekend/experience usually means self-drive.

---

## Section A — Self-drive questions

Ask in this order:

1. **Which vehicle?** (or suggest based on occasion/budget)
2. **Start date**
3. **End date** (used to work out the number of days)
4. **Driver's age** (self-drive has a minimum age — typically **23–25+**
   depending on the car; confirm the driver qualifies)
5. **Collection from Birmingham, or delivery to you?** (delivery is priced by
   location, from £50 locally)

**Then quote:** self-drive uses tiered day/3-day/week/month rates (best combination
is chosen automatically — e.g. 9 days = 1 week + 2 days). Give the indicative total
and confirm.

---

## Section B — Chauffeur questions

Ask in this order (mirrors the website's chauffeur quote form):

1. **Occasion / event type** — Wedding · Prom · Airport transfer · Corporate /
   business · Night out / event · Other.
   *(Context only — it does not change the price, but it helps us route and prepare.)*
2. **Which vehicle?** — Rolls-Royce Phantom · Rolls-Royce Ghost · Rolls-Royce
   Cullinan · Lamborghini Urus · Mercedes-AMG G 63 (G-Wagon) · Mercedes V-Class
   (up to 8 passengers). *(All seat up to 4 except the V-Class, which seats up to 8.)*
3. **One-way or return?**
4. **Date of hire.**
5. **Pick-up time** (and **return time** if it's a return trip).
6. **If it's an airport transfer:** which **airport**? (This becomes the drop-off;
   for arrivals, swap pick-up/drop-off.)
7. **Pick-up location/postcode** and **drop-off location/postcode.**
   *(Used to work out the **reach** — the furthest point from Birmingham — which
   drives the price. See the pricing guide.)*
8. **If it's a return within ~30 miles of Birmingham:** should the **car drop you
   and collect later**, or **wait with you** the whole time?
   - Beyond ~30 miles, or if they want the car to stay, it's automatically a
     **waited chauffeured day** (covers up to 8 hours; extra hours charged per hour).
9. **How many passengers?** (check it's within the vehicle's limit — max 4, or 8
   for the V-Class)
10. **Any extra stops?** (each adds a small per-stop fee)
11. **Any special requests?** — ribbons, champagne, child seat, a specific route, etc.

**Then quote:** apply the matching chauffeur formula (one-way / drop & collect /
waited day) from the pricing guide, add extra hours (day only), London premium
(day only, if pick-up or drop-off is in London), and any stops. Present as an
indicative "from" price.

---

## Step 2 — Close and hand over

1. Give the **indicative "from" price** and a one-line breakdown if helpful.
2. Reassure: *"That's a guide — our team will confirm the final price and can often
   tailor it to your booking."*
3. Confirm we have their **name + mobile/email**, and the **date**.
4. Let them know the team will be in touch to finalise, or they can call
   **0121 572 3422**.

---

## Quick checklist — details to capture for a chauffeur lead

- [ ] Name
- [ ] Mobile and/or email
- [ ] Occasion / event type
- [ ] Vehicle
- [ ] One-way or return
- [ ] Date + pick-up time (+ return time)
- [ ] Pick-up and drop-off (for reach / distance)
- [ ] Car waits vs drop & collect (return, local)
- [ ] Passengers
- [ ] Extra stops
- [ ] Special requests

## Quick checklist — details to capture for a self-drive lead

- [ ] Name
- [ ] Mobile and/or email
- [ ] Vehicle
- [ ] Start and end date
- [ ] Driver's age (meets the minimum for that car)
- [ ] Collection or delivery (delivery location)

---

### Notes
- Nationwide UK delivery/coverage is available subject to location.
- If a customer is unsure of exact postcodes or times, take their best estimate —
  the team confirms exact mileage and timings.
- Opening hours: Mon–Fri 10:00–17:00, Sat–Sun 11:00–14:00. Outside hours, capture
  the lead and let them know the team will reply.
